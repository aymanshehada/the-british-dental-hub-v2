Modern.
Clean.
Elegant.
Luxury.
Professional.

Not flashy.

# Design Principles

Lots of white space.

Large typography.

Minimal design.

Rounded corners.

Soft shadows.

Real photography.

No fake luxury.

No black backgrounds.

No gold gradients.

Everything should feel calm and premium.

---

# Photography

Always use real clinic photos whenever possible.

Priority:

1. Reception
2. Treatment rooms
3. Equipment
4. Team
5. Patients

Avoid generic stock photos.
